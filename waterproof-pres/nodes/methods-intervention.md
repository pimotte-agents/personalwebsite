# Intervention

- Both teachers introduced Waterproof in the first lesson; Otte also presented selected proofs live in Waterproof in the first weeks
- Examples and exercises in Waterproof, mostly developed by Van Tilburg in his MSc thesis [@tilburg2025waterproof]
- Waterproof: an educational proof assistant based on the Rocq Prover
  - students write proofs in a controlled natural language
  - they can ask for help, and get feedback that enforces a particular style of proof ([screenshot](#fig-screenshot))
- Use of Waterproof was voluntary
