# Mizar

[Website](https://mizar.uwb.edu.pl/)

- Proof checker started by Andrzej Trybulec in 1973 [@grabowski2010mizar]
- Its language is designed to be close to the mathematical vernacular
- Declarative proofs in Jaśkowski-style natural deduction: `let`, `assume`, `thus`, `consider` [@grabowski2010mizar]
- The Mizar Mathematical Library: one of the oldest large libraries of formalised mathematics
