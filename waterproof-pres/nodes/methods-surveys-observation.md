# Surveys and observation

- Entry survey: secondary school grades, study programme, gender, age
- Exit survey: time spent, perception of feedback (Likert scale), reasons to continue or stop using Waterproof
  - totals under 20 hours were read as hours per week and multiplied by the 8 weeks of the course
  - open coding of the free-text answers, each with a valence: helping, deterring or neither
- Observation of Otte's class and one class without Waterproof, with teachers of similar experience
  - each observer followed 4 students, at least every 5 minutes, in the last 2 h 45 min of a session
  - time on the course, on other mathematics, on unrelated tasks, or ambiguous; and all questions asked
