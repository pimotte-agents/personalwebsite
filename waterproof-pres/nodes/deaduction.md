# D∃∀duction

[Sources](https://github.com/dEAduction/dEAduction)

- Graphical proof assistant on top of Lean, by Frédéric Le Roux and others [@kerjean2022l1]
- Students build proofs by selecting hypotheses and clicking on logical actions
- Shows the context and goal in mathematical notation
- Used in a first-year course on mathematical reasoning at Sorbonne Université
