# Design principles for Lean-based learning environments

Garnelo & Liebendörfer, design-based research at Paderborn [@garnelo2025design]

- A learning environment with Lean exercises and written exercise sheets, for preservice teachers
- Design principles derived from theory and a study on their prototype:
  - Scaffold: exercises on parts of a proof, so students can learn the basics of Lean first
  - Draw students' attention to Lean's output
  - Interweave the written exercises with the Lean tasks
