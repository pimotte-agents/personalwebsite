# Proof structure after Waterproof

Wemmenhove, Hoofd, Schüler-Meyer & Portegies [@wemmenhove2026comparative]

- Compares the structure of proofs by students who did and didn't use Waterproof
- Waterproof users:
  - introduced variables more often
  - announced the goal to be proven more often
  - but forgot to check domain conditions in quantifiers more often (traced to Waterproof, since improved)
