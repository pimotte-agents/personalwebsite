# Setup

- First-year course *Bewijzen in de Wiskunde* at Utrecht University: seven parallel groups with different teachers, shared materials and assessment
- Waterproof (the Rocq version) offered in 2 of the 7 groups (Commelin, Otte); use was voluntary
- Research participation was opt-in
- A quasi-experiment in a realistic setting: students were not randomly assigned to Waterproof
- Four groups: active Waterproof users (≥ 5 exercises), inactive users, use unknown, and a control group from the other 5 groups
