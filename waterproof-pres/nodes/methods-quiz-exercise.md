# The exercise in the first quiz

> Let $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ be predicates with a free variable $x \in \mathbb{R}$.\
> Prove that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, if
> $$p(x) \Rightarrow \bigl((\neg q(x) \Rightarrow q(3)) \vee q(14)\bigr)$$
> then
> $$\bigl(\neg p(x)\bigr) \vee \bigl(\exists y \in \mathbb{R}, q(y)\bigr)$$

- A pretest with a similar exercise was given at the start of the course, before the material was taught
- Grades on the Dutch scale from 1 to 10; 5.5 or higher is a pass
