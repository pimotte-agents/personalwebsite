# Naproche

[Website](https://naproche.github.io/)

- Natural Proof Checking, from Bonn [@cramer2010naproche]
- Checks mathematical texts written in a controlled natural language, typeset in LaTeX
- Automated theorem provers fill in the steps between sentences
- Today Naproche checks texts in ForTheL, the language of the System for Automated Deduction (SAD)
