# Limitations

- Teacher effect: Otte and Commelin, both influenced by proof assistants, taught the Waterproof groups
- Quasi-experiment: students self-selected into using Waterproof, and the groups partly follow study programme
- Use of Waterproof is unknown for 11 of the 34 students in the Waterproof groups
- The open coding of the survey was done by a single researcher
