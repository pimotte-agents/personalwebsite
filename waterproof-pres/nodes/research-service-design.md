# Service design for Waterproof

Wemmenhove, Bór, Conijn & Portegies [@wemmenhove2025service]

- A service design approach to developing an intelligent tutoring system, with Waterproof as the case
- Three substudies: a co-creative service blueprinting session, a usability test and an analysis of trace data
- Concrete improvements, e.g. to onboarding and to the error messages
- Looks beyond the tool: all stakeholders and the course around it
