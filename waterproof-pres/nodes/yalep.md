# Yalep

[Talk](https://europroofnet.github.io/_pages/WG3/Sept2025/MCLP-2025-tran-minh-gonnord-narboux-yalep.pdf)

- An environment for learning proof in high school, by Frédéric Tran Minh, Laure Gonnord and Julien Narboux [@tranminh2025yalep]
- A tiny language on top of Lean, based on forward chaining
- High-school proofs are short, and introduce connectives and quantifiers without formal symbols
