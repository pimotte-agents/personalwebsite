# Exercises that don't fit

*Where "close to paper" runs out: textbook exercises without a direct formal counterpart.*

- **Always, sometimes or never?** Three parts; only the correct one has a proof.
- **True or false?** Two parts, `(true)` and `(false)`; again only one has a proof.
- **Find a formula:** dropped, because stating the formula gives the answer away.
- **Find the image or preimage:** the answer is stated as a set equality to prove.
- **Truth tables:** proved with ordinary proof steps instead.
