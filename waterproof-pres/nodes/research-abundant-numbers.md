# Learning about proof with Lean

Thoma & Iannone: the abundant numbers task [@thoma2022learning]

- Voluntary Lean workshops alongside a transition-to-proof module in the UK
- Qualitative analysis of 36 student proofs of an unfamiliar statement
- Students who engaged with Lean:
  - used mathematical language and symbols more accurately, in complete sentences
  - broke their proofs down into goals and sub-goals explicitly
