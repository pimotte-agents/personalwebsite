# What do you have to trust?

A kernel-checked proof of a statement $S$ tells you: *$S$ follows from Lean's axioms*. To believe the mathematics, you also need:

- **The definitions** used in $S$, all the way down to Lean's axioms
- **The statement**: $S$ says what the informal statement says
- **Mathlib**, as a stand-in for checking those definitions yourself
- **Lean**: its logic, its kernel, and the software around the kernel
- **The checking setup**: that what was checked is what you think was checked
